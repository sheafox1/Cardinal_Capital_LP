Why Cardinal Capital Group?

Cardinal Capital Group is a reliable private money lender with an exceptional track record in the industry. Our mission is to use our proven methodology and in-house financial expertise to support our clients, with professional advice and insight so they can make informed decisions about their lending options.

We offer a diverse range of lending products tailored to the needs of each client, and we work to be the capital partner you can trust.

Interest as disbursed

On construction, the money releases in draws as the work gets done. With CCG, interest follows the draws, so you are not paying on funds still sitting in the schedule. On a long build, that difference shows up directly in your returns.
